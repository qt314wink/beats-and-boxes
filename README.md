## 🎵 What Is Beats & Boxes?

Beats & Boxes is a community initiative rooted in Philadelphia's EDM and electronic music scene. It exists at the intersection of:

- **Live music infrastructure** — DIY venue activation, sound system access, promoter support
- **Regenerative systems** — circular economy models for gear, space-sharing, ecological awareness
- **Creative community building** — connecting producers, DJs, visual artists, and venue operators across Philly

This repo serves as the **living planning document, grant toolkit, and initiative framework** for Beats & Boxes.

---

## 🏛️ Philly Scene Context

Philadelphia has a rich but under-resourced underground EDM scene. Beats & Boxes targets:

- **FringeArts & surrounding Old City** — experimental and electronic crossover
- **Fishtown / Kensington corridor** — DIY warehouse venue ecosystem
- **North Philly** — emerging producer communities and community studios
- **South Philly** — latinx-influenced electronic and dance music communities

## 🌱 Regenerative Principles

Beats & Boxes isn't just a party series — it's built on regenerative infrastructure:

1. **Gear Library** — Community-owned sound equipment circulates between events and artists
2. **Revenue Recycling** — % of door revenue funds the next community producer's studio session
3. **Venue Ecology** — Partner with existing spaces vs. building new; reduce carbon footprint
4. **Compost the Scene** — Connect with Philly urban ag orgs for post-event composting
