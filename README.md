## 🌱 What Is Beats & Boxes?

Beats & Boxes is a regenerative creative initiative rooted in Philadelphia. It brings together the EDM and local music community with systems-based design thinking — creating infrastructure for live events, sound production workshops, community recording access, and maker-style music spaces.

The initiative operates on the principle that **music production is a form of community infrastructure** — as essential to a neighborhood as parks or libraries.

---

## 🎯 Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|---|---|
| 📦 **The Box** | Modular mobile music studio — a physical kit that can be set up anywhere |
| 🔊 **The Beat** | Workshop series teaching production, sampling, and sound design |
| 🌐 **The Network** | Community platform connecting producers, venues, and listeners |
| 🌿 **The Regenerative Loop** | Revenue and resources cycle back into the community |

---

## 📅 Programming

- **Monthly Beat Battles** — open to all skill levels
- **Production Workshops** — DAW fundamentals, sampling, synthesis
- **Community Listening Sessions** — critique, feedback, and celebration
- **Pop-Up Stages** — partnering with Philly venues and festivals
