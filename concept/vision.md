# Beats & Boxes — Vision Document

## The Problem

Music production tools, studio time, and mentorship are not equitably distributed in Philadelphia. Talented producers in under-resourced neighborhoods lack access to the infrastructure that gets people heard.

## The Vision

Beats & Boxes imagines a city where every neighborhood has access to a **Box** — a fully-equipped, portable music studio that can activate any space: a rec center, a library, a parking lot, a community garden.

It's not just equipment. It's curriculum, community, and a network of people who show up for each other.

## Core Values

- **Access over gatekeeping** — no auditions, no barriers
- **Process over product** — learning is the point
- **Community as infrastructure** — people are the real resource
- **Regenerative economics** — resources cycle back in, not out

## Theory of Change

When people have access to creative tools + community + mentorship:
1. They make things
2. Those things build identity and confidence
3. That confidence spills into other parts of life
4. The community gets stronger
