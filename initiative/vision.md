# Beats & Boxes — Vision & Theory of Change

## Mission
To build a self-sustaining, regenerative creative ecosystem in Philadelphia that centers electronic music as community infrastructure — not just entertainment.

## Vision
A Philadelphia where every neighborhood has access to live electronic music, sound education, and community-owned creative space.

## Theory of Change

```
Inputs:
  - Community events (monthly/quarterly)
  - Gear library (donated/shared equipment)
  - Artist stipends (from door revenue)
  - Grant funding (PCF, Art Works, CREJ-adjacent)

Activities:
  - EDM showcases and warehouse events
  - Producer mentorship sessions
  - DJ workshops for youth
  - Venue activation partnerships

Outputs:
  - # of events produced
  - # of artists supported
  - $ circulated back to community
  - # of youth reached

Outcomes:
  - Increased neighborhood creative density
  - New venue partnerships activated
  - Self-sustaining gear library operational

Impact:
  - Philadelphia recognized as East Coast EDM community hub
  - Regenerative model replicated in other cities
```

## Guiding Principles
1. **Community first** — every decision centers the artist and the neighbor
2. **Regenerative by default** — every system feeds the next cycle
3. **Radically accessible** — low/no-cost entry, stipends, gear access
4. **Joyful** — we are building the world we want to live in, right now
