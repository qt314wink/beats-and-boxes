# Grant Research — Beats & Boxes

## Active Opportunities

### Philadelphia Cultural Fund
- **Deadline**: Rolling / Annual
- **Amount**: Up to $10,000
- **Fit**: Community arts programming, access
- **Status**: Research phase

### Pew Center for Arts & Heritage
- **Deadline**: Annual (varies)
- **Amount**: $25,000–$150,000
- **Fit**: Deep Philadelphia cultural projects with research component
- **Status**: Research phase

### William Penn Foundation
- **Focus**: Arts & Culture, Greater Philadelphia
- **Fit**: Community-centered creative initiatives
- **Status**: Research phase

### CREJ (Community Revitalization & Equitable Jobs)
- **Focus**: Economic opportunity, community infrastructure
- **Fit**: Beats & Boxes as job creation + community space
- **Status**: Research phase

### Art Works (NEA)
- **Deadline**: Annual
- **Amount**: $10,000–$100,000
- **Fit**: Projects of national artistic significance with community benefit
- **Status**: Research phase

## Notes

- Lead with the **community access** angle first, creative tech second
- Emphasize Philadelphia roots and local partnerships
- Quantify reach: workshops attended, producers trained, events held
